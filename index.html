About the studio

Dormroom Studios is a small independent game studio: a team of three in India. We make single-player, story-led games for PC, and we started with one mission: to make games that stay with the people who play them.

Neon Fragments is our first major release. It comes to Steam for Windows on 25 February 2027, and everything we have goes into it until it ships.

We’re just getting started. Thank you for being part of the journey from the very beginning.

Neon Fragments

First-person shooter · Steam, Windows · 25 February 2027

Neon Fragments is a first-person shooter set in a dystopian near future. In a world of despair and suffering, the ones you can call your own are your everything. Neon Fragments is a game about loss, about love, about actions and about hope.
