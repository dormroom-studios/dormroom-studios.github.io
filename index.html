About

Three people in India, making single-player games for PC.

Dormroom Studios is a small team. We make story-led, single-player games, the kind that stay with you after you’ve put them down.

Neon Fragments is our first major release: a first-person shooter about a lone drifter in a fractured city, coming to Steam on 25 February 2027. Until it ships, it’s all we’re doing.

Neon Fragments

A first-person shooter about loss, love and hope.

Wars took the innocent. Rising seas took everything else. You are a lone drifter in a fractured city, piecing the story together from phone calls and the places you pass through. In a world of despair and suffering, the ones you can call your own are your everything.
